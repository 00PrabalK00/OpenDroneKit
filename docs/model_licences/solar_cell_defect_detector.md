- **Use:** non-commercial only (PVEL-AD is released for research and academic purposes),
  and the weights are also AGPL-3.0 (an Ultralytics YOLO11l fine-tune). See the OWNER GATE
  in `WEIGHTS_LICENSES.md`: AGPL-3.0 and a non-commercial restriction cannot both be
  satisfied. Shipped flagged, with both notices, pending the owner's decision.
- **Weights:** AGPL-3.0 (https://www.ultralytics.com/license) and the PVEL-AD terms. Full
  texts: `LICENSES/solar_cell_defect_detector.txt`.
- **Decision under the owner's rule:** ship. The published PVEL-AD terms restrict use to
  research and academic purposes. They do not forbid distributing a trained model.

## Training data and its terms, as read on 2026-10-03

PVEL-AD (Hebei University of Technology and Beihang University),
https://github.com/binyisu/PVEL-AD. The repository's Apache-2.0 LICENSE covers the code.
The data is released through the "Industrial Datasets Request Form"
(`Industrial_Data_Access_Form.docx` in that repository), which says:

> The datasets can ONLY be used for research and academic purposes.

The form also has the requester accept "the data license/agreement document". No such
separate agreement is published in the repository or on the request page; the form is the
only text of the terms found. Since 2026-01-30 the README also links a public Google Drive
download. If the agreement document is ever published and forbids distributing derived
models, this asset must be withdrawn.
